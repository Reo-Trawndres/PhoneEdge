VoxLingua107 ECAPA-TDNN language ID (lang-id-voxlingua107-ecapa-Q8_0.gguf: which language a dictation is in, for hold to write
with Cohere Arabic; not part of PhoneEdge's download: fetched only when you click Download in Settings > Voice & AI > Speed or
accuracy > Cohere Arabic) - https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/lang-id-voxlingua107-ecapa, by SpeechBrain.

PhoneEdge fetches a GGUF file converted from SpeechBrain's checkpoint and quantized to 8 bits (Q8_0) by the makers of
transcribe.cpp (https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/lang-id-voxlingua107-ecapa-gguf, public, no sign-in), at a fixed revision,
checked against a pinned SHA-256, and keeps it per user in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhoneEdge\models. It is data: PhoneEdge never runs it
as code. It is run by transcribe.cpp (see transcribe.cpp-LICENSE.txt), on the processor.
