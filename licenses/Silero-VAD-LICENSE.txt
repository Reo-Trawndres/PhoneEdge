Silero VAD v6.2.0 (ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin: the voice detector that trims each recording to its speech for Cohere Arabic;
not part of PhoneEdge's download: fetched only when you click Download in Settings > Voice & AI > Speed or accuracy > Cohere
Arabic) - https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad, in whisper.cpp's ggml format from
https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad, checked against a pinned SHA-256.
